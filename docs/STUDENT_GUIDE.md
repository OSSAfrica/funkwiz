# Student guide

[Documentation index](README.md) · [Troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md)

You need a browser, an internet connection and an invitation from the teacher.
You do not need a Funkwiz account, password, Google login or teacher setup code.

## Join a game

1. Open the join link your teacher shares, or scan the lobby QR code with your camera.
2. If needed, enter the six-digit game code.
3. Choose a nickname your teacher will recognize, then select **Join the fun**.
4. Wait in the lobby while everyone connects. The teacher starts the game.

Nicknames must be unique within the room. If yours is taken, choose another.
Follow your teacher's naming instructions and avoid sharing unnecessary personal
information. Nicknames and results can appear in the saved teacher report.

New participants cannot join after the game starts. Ask the teacher for help if
you arrive late. A game code is an invitation, not a password-reset or teacher
registration code.

## Answer questions

Select one of the four answer buttons before time runs out. Your first answer
locks by default. If your teacher allows changes, the feedback says **Answer
saved** and you can tap another choice before time ends or the teacher reveals.
Your latest choice and its submission time determine your points; an early
incorrect choice does not reserve a speed bonus. The teacher can change this rule between questions.
The correct answer appears when the teacher
reveals it or the question timer ends.

The teacher decides where the question appears. If you see only answer buttons,
look at the shared screen. When the teacher pauses, answering is disabled until
the game resumes. New games rank by **most correct answers first**. Each correct
answer earns 700–1,000 time-based points; faster correct answers earn more.
With equal correct-answer counts, higher points win. Precise timing breaks remaining
ties; identical results share a rank. Wrong or unanswered questions earn 0.
Points appear at reveal, and paused time does not count. Earlier games may show
different rules; read the note in your lobby or report. Open **Help & scoring**
or read the [FAQ](FAQ.md) and [scoring examples](SCORING.md).

You can choose a light/dark appearance and control audio on your device. Music
and effects try to start automatically, including in the lobby and results. If
your browser blocks autoplay, choose **Enable audio** or **Keep it quiet**. Your
mute, volume and clock-tick choices are saved for the next game in this browser.
A silent device does not stop you answering.

Finish in the top three to see a short confetti celebration, including shared
ranks. It plays once per game in this tab. Reduced-motion settings replace the
celebration with a still result.

## Reconnect safely

If the connection drops, wait for reconnect feedback or refresh in the same
browser. Your room identity and latest saved answers are restored using a private
browser cookie. Do not clear cookies or switch browsers if you want to keep that
identity. Opening the same game session in another tab replaces the older connection.

Keep the game visible when you can. Question timers are controlled by the server
and do not stop when you switch tabs or lose connectivity. Your device needs a
connection to submit an answer before the deadline.

## After the game

The room shows the final results. Saved report downloads belong to the teacher;
ask them if you need feedback. There is no student account history or homework
assignment mode in the current app.

If the link does not open, a code is rejected, or reconnecting does not work,
follow [troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md#joining-and-live-games) and tell your
teacher what you see. Never send anyone the teacher's private setup code.

## Share your result

After the game, choose **Share my result** to download a colorful PNG with your
nickname, quiz title, rank out of the total players, points and correct-answer count. Share that image
wherever you like. It contains no room code or other players’ names.
